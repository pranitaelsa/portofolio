Journey

My path as a content creator

January 2025

A quiet beginning

It started small: a few light jogs just to get moving. No plan and no audience, only a first step toward feeling better in my own body.

April 2025

From a diet goal to a lifestyle

What began as a wish to lose weight became a real commitment. I went from 76 kg to 62 kg, and I’m still maintaining it today. I joined a gym and tried class after class, exploring as many kinds of sport as I could.

May 2025

Filming for myself

I fell in love with making my own workout videos. At first I only shared them on Instagram and TikTok stories, with no pressure, just documenting the process honestly.

June 2026

The first offer

A pilates studio invited me to review their space, my first collaboration. It was proof that honest, everyday content could mean something to brands too.

June 2026 – Now

Consistent, focused, growing

Since then I’ve been creating content consistently, focused on sport and wellness: pilates, yoga, and gym. Today that means 30+ brand collaborations, a 16.6% average engagement rate, and 100K+ views within 30 days.
